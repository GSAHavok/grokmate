    How to get a free key

    Both take about 2 minutes. Free tiers have limits (requests per minute and per day). If you hit a limit, wait a bit or switch provider.

    Google Gemini (free tier)

    1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
    2. Tap Create API key (accept the terms if asked).
    3. Tap Copy next to the new key.
    4. In GrokMate: Settings → Provider Google Gemini → paste the key → Save & test.

    Google may use free-tier prompts to improve its products. Don't share private details on the free tier.

    Groq (free tier)

    1. Open console.groq.com/keys and sign up (email or Google).
    2. Tap Create API Key, give it a name like "GrokMate", and submit.
    3. Copy the key right away (it's shown only once).
    4. In GrokMate: Settings → Provider Groq → paste the key → Save & test.

    Want Grok itself?

    Create a key at console.x.ai. xAI bills you directly for what you use (pay as you go). GrokMate never charges for AI.

    Keep your key private. It's stored only on this phone; to remove it, clear it in Settings.

    About GrokMate Mobile

    GrokMate Mobile

    Independent project. Not affiliated with xAI.

    What it can do

    • Chat by voice or text with your own AI key (xAI Grok, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter or OpenAI).
    • Timers that ring with sound and vibration while GrokMate is open.
    • Notes saved on this phone (search, delete, export).
    • Reminders as calendar events: tap "Add to calendar" and your phone's calendar alerts you, even when GrokMate is closed.
    • Buttons to call, text, email, open maps or search. Nothing happens until you tap.
    • Weather (Open-Meteo), copy & share, date, time and maths.

    What it can't do

    It can't control other apps, set system alarms, read your contacts or messages, or listen in the background (no wake word). It doesn't control your PC; for that, use the phone remote in GrokMate for Windows ("pair a phone").

    Privacy

    • Your chats, notes, timers, settings and API key are stored only on this phone.
    • Your messages go straight from this phone to the AI provider you chose, under that provider's terms. There's no GrokMate server.
    • Your license key is checked with Gumroad. Weather lookups go to Open-Meteo (your location only if you ask for local weather).
    • Voice input uses your browser's speech service (Chrome sends audio to Google; Safari uses Apple). Spoken replies use your phone's voices.
